A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before you get started.

What can I track in Obligio?

Business and trade licenses, permits, insurance renewals, filing deadlines, safety inspections, staff certifications and any other dated business obligation you enter.

Does Obligio tell me every requirement for my business?

No. The suggested catalogue covers selected US federal obligations. You decide which apply, add your own obligations and verify requirements and dates with official sources or a qualified adviser.

Can I download the app now?

Yes—download Obligio for Android on Google Play. Availability depends on your Google Play country and device. The iPhone app is awaiting App Store review.

How do I get support or manage my account?

Visit Obligio Support for help with reminders, purchases and account deletion. Our privacy policy explains how account details and uploaded documents are handled.
