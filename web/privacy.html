Privacy policy

Obligio helps small businesses track compliance obligations. This page explains what the app collects, who processes it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

Account details. When you sign in, our authentication provider records your email address and issues an account identifier. Obligio stores that identifier against your business so we can show you your own records and nobody else's.

What you enter. Your business name, location and industry, the obligations you track, their dates and categories, and any documents you upload as evidence.

Subscription status. If you subscribe, our payments provider records which plan you hold and whether it is active. Card details are handled by Apple or Google and never reach us.

What we do not collect

Obligio contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting software. We do not track you across other apps or websites, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or share personal information with data brokers.

Who processes your data

We use a small number of providers to run the service: an authentication provider for sign-in, a backend and file-storage provider for your records and uploaded documents, and a subscription provider for billing status. Each processes data only to deliver Obligio to you. Reminders are scheduled on your own device and their contents are not sent to us.

How long we keep it

Your records are kept while your account is open. Deleting an obligation deletes any document attached to it. Ask us to close your account and we delete your business records and documents.

Your choices

Request access, correction, export, or deletion of your account and associated data by emailing privacy@obligio.com. You can turn deadline reminders off at any time in your device settings.

Children

Obligio is a tool for business owners and is not directed at children.
