Compliance software for small business: which kind you need, what it costs, and when a spreadsheet is enough

"Compliance software" is sold for four different jobs, and most small businesses need only one or two of them. If your worry is license, permit and insurance deadlines, you need a deadline tracker; a free spreadsheet works for about ten items. State annual reports are handled by a registered agent service; Northwest charges $125 a year. Payroll tax filings are handled by your payroll provider; Gusto files Forms 941, 940 and W-2. Only if a customer asks you for SOC 2 or HIPAA evidence do you need a security platform such as Vanta or Drata, and neither publishes prices. The table below sorts the jobs, with prices checked on 16 November 2026.

Deadline trackers, for licenses, permits and insurance

This is knowing that the city license expires on 30 April and the liability policy on 1 July, and being told early enough to act. Here the cheapest option works longest.

  • A spreadsheet. Free, but a dated cell reminds no one. Our compliance tracking spreadsheet guide has the formulas, color rules and a short Google Sheets script that emails you when a date is near.
  • Expiration Reminder. A dedicated expiry tracker. Starter is $49 a month for 150 items, Professional $99 for 600. Every plan includes unlimited admin users and file storage, SMS reminders, iOS and Android apps, and a 14-day trial with no card. It suits a business tracking many people's certificates, vehicles or sites, where more than one person needs the list.
  • Harbor Compliance License Manager. It tracks the "status and renewals of firm and individual licenses", stores documents against each license and adds a database of licensing requirements by state. Harbor says it is built for "compliance professionals and administrative teams". No price is published; you book a demo. It suits a firm licensed in many states, not a single-location shop.

For what to record against each license and when to set reminders, see the business license renewal tracker.

Registered agents, for state annual reports

An LLC or corporation must keep a registered agent and, in most states, file an annual or biennial report. Northwest warns that a missed report can cost good standing. A registered agent service receives the state's mail. Northwest charges $125 a year, or $100 a year per state for five or more states. It says it will remind you as the deadline approaches and "take care of the requirement for you". Ask what each filing costs before relying on that. You can be your own agent if you have an address in the state and are there in business hours; then the report date is one more row in your tracker. Federal and annual dates by entity type are in the small business compliance calendar.

Payroll providers, for payroll tax filings

With employees, your most frequent deadlines are payroll tax deposits and quarterly returns. A payroll provider files these; no tracker does. Gusto's pricing page says it files "federal, state, and local payroll tax forms on your company's behalf (things like Form W-2, Form 1099, Form 940, Form 941, Form 8974, and others)". Simple costs $49 a month plus $6 per person; Plus and Premium cost more. Its Business Compliance add-on, which tracks "state tax registrations, annual reports, and agency mail", is $85 a month per state on an annual contract.

Compliance automation platforms, for security audits

Vanta and Drata automate evidence for security and privacy frameworks. Vanta lists SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA and others. Drata's Foundation plan is "Limited to SOC 2, ISO 27001, Cyber Essentials, HIPAA, and GDPR". Vanta offers "personalized pricing" after a demo; Drata's plans also lead to a demo, with no price shown. You need one when a customer's contract or security questionnaire asks for a SOC 2 report, or a regulator expects one. If nobody has asked, this row is not yours.

A decision rule you can apply today

  1. List and count every dated obligation: licenses, permits, policies, certificates, reports, filings.
  2. Payroll? Use a payroll provider for the tax filings, whatever else you decide.
  3. An LLC or corporation in more than one state, or no reliable in-state address? Use a registered agent service. One state with an address? Track the report date yourself.
  4. For the licenses and policies left: under about ten items and one person responsible, a spreadsheet with reminders or a phone tracker. More items or more people, a team tracker such as Expiration Reminder. Many states, ask Harbor for a demo.
  5. A customer asked for SOC 2, ISO 27001 or HIPAA evidence? Then, and only then, get quotes from Vanta and Drata.

Five questions to ask before you pay for any of them

  • Does it file anything, or only remind you? Most trackers only remind.
  • Is it priced per item, person, state, or by quote? Price it at next year's size.
  • Who gets the reminder, how, and can a second person get it?
  • Can you attach the certificate to the deadline, so the proof sits with the date?
  • Can you export your list if you cancel? A tracker you cannot leave is a new risk.

When this page does not apply

  • You do not yet know which licenses you need. Start with the SBA's licenses and permits guide and your state's business portal, then come back to choose a tracker.
  • A license has already lapsed. See what happens if a business license expires and fix that before buying anything.
  • You are in a regulated field such as healthcare, financial services or cannabis. The rules there are specific; ask a licensed attorney or a compliance adviser in that field.
  • Your company is Australian. See the ASIC annual review date guide.

Questions

What is the cheapest compliance software for a small business?

A spreadsheet with a reminder script costs nothing and works for about ten dated obligations tracked by one person. Of the paid tools checked on 16 November 2026, Northwest's registered agent service is $125 a year and Expiration Reminder starts at $528 a year billed annually. Harbor Compliance, Vanta and Drata publish no prices.

Do I need SOC 2 compliance software?

Only if a customer, contract or regulator asks you for a SOC 2 report or similar evidence. Platforms such as Vanta and Drata exist for that job and quote prices after a demo. A business that only needs to keep licenses and insurance current does not need one.
