STATE FILING · DELAWARE

Delaware annual report due date

Annual Report and Franchise Tax (domestic corporations): On or before March 1 each year, covering the prior year; must be filed online.
Annual Report (foreign corporations): On or before June 30 each year.
Annual tax for LLCs, LPs and GPs (no annual report): Pay the annual tax for the prior year on or before June 1 each year.

From the official Delaware Division of Corporations page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Annual Report and Franchise Tax (domestic corporations)

Who files it

Active domestic (Delaware) corporations.

The official page says: “All active Domestic Corporation Annual Reports and Franchise Taxes” (Delaware Division of Corporations — Annual Report and Tax Instructions)

When it is due

On or before March 1 each year, covering the prior year; must be filed online. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “are due annually on or before March 1st and are required to be filed online” (Delaware Division of Corporations — Annual Report and Tax Instructions)

What it costs

Annual report filing fee: $50 for non-exempt domestic corporations, $25 for exempt domestic corporations (effective September 1, 2019). Franchise tax is extra: minimum $175 (Authorized Shares Method) or $400 (Assumed Par Value Capital Method), maximum $200,000 ($250,000 for Large Corporate Filers).

The official page says: “Exempt Domestic Corporations – $25.00. Non-Exempt Domestic Corporations – $50.00.” (Delaware Division of Corporations — Annual Report and Tax Instructions)

If it is late

$200 penalty plus 1.5% interest per month on tax and penalty.

The official page says: “a penalty of $200.00 plus 1.5% interest per month on tax and penalty” (Delaware Division of Corporations — Annual Report and Tax Instructions)

File online through the state's filing service.

Annual Report (foreign corporations)

Who files it

Foreign corporations registered in Delaware.

The official page says: “Foreign Corporations must file an Annual Report” (Delaware Division of Corporations — Annual Report and Tax Instructions)

When it is due

On or before June 30 each year. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “Delaware Secretary of State on or before June 30 each year” (Delaware Division of Corporations — Annual Report and Tax Instructions)

What it costs

$250 filing fee.

The official page says: “A $250.00 filing fee is required to be paid.” (Delaware Division of Corporations — Annual Report and Tax Instructions)

If it is late

$250 penalty added to the filing fee if the report and payment are not received by the due date.

The official page says: “a $250.00 penalty will be added to filing fee” (Delaware Division of Corporations — Annual Report and Tax Instructions)

File online through the state's filing service.

Annual tax for LLCs, LPs and GPs (no annual report)

Who files it

Domestic and foreign LLCs, limited partnerships and general partnerships formed or registered in Delaware. They file no annual report.

The official page says: “All Domestic and Foreign Limited Liability Companies, Limited Partnerships, and General Partnerships” (Delaware Division of Corporations — LLC/LP/GP Franchise Tax Instructions)

When it is due

Pay the annual tax for the prior year on or before June 1 each year. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “The annual taxes for the prior year are due on or before June 1st.” (Delaware Division of Corporations — LLC/LP/GP Franchise Tax Instructions)

What it costs

$400 annual tax (no proration).

The official page says: “they must pay the $400 yearly tax on or before June 1st” (Delaware Division of Corporations — LLC/LP/GP Franchise Tax Instructions)

If it is late

$200 penalty plus 1.5% interest per month on tax and penalty.

The official page says: “Failure to pay the required annual taxes will result in a penalty of $200.00” (Delaware Division of Corporations — LLC/LP/GP Franchise Tax Instructions)

File online through the state's filing service.
