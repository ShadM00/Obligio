STATE FILING · NORTH DAKOTA

North Dakota annual report due date

Annual Report (domestic business corporations and domestic professional corporations): On or before August 1 each year, starting the year after the year of registration.
Annual Report (foreign business corporations and foreign professional corporations): On or before May 15 each year, starting the year after the year of registration.
Annual Report (limited liability companies (LLC) and professional limited liability…): On or before November 15 each year, starting the year after the year of registration.
Annual Report (farming or ranching LLCs and corporations, and authorized livestock farm…): On or before April 15 each year, starting the year after the year of registration.
Annual Report (limited partnerships, limited liability partnerships, limited liability…): On or before March 31 each year, starting the year after the year of registration.

From the official North Dakota Secretary of State page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Annual Report (domestic business corporations and domestic professional corporations)

Who files it

Domestic business corporations and domestic professional corporations.

The official page says: “August 1 Business Corporation (Domestic) Professional Corporation (Domestic)” (North Dakota Secretary of State — Maintain Registration)

When it is due

On or before August 1 each year, starting the year after the year of registration. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “August 1 Business Corporation (Domestic) Professional Corporation (Domestic)” (North Dakota Secretary of State — Maintain Registration)

What it costs

The state's page we read does not state the fee. The state shows it when you file.

If it is late

A late filing fee applies if the report is not received on or before the deadline. The amount is not stated on the state's page. The business is placed in Not Good Standing. Typically it is involuntarily terminated if the past-due report is not filed within 6 to 12 months (a foreign business has its authority revoked). The state's page also says that if the report is not filed within 1 year the business will be involuntarily dissolved or terminated.

The official page says: “on or before the deadline or incur a late filing fee” (North Dakota Secretary of State — Maintain Registration)

File online through the state's filing service.
