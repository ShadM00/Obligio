STATE FILING · UTAH

Utah annual report due date

Annual report for division: Each calendar year by the last day of the entity's anniversary month: the month its formation became effective with the Division or, for a foreign entity, the month its authority to conduct affairs in Utah took effect. It may be filed up to 60 days before that date. The Division may set a different period by rule.

From the official Utah Legislature page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Who has to file the Utah Annual report for division?

Each domestic filing entity and registered foreign entity. The statute does not name entity types such as corporations or LLCs.

The official page says: “Each domestic filing entity and registered foreign entity shall file an annual report” (Utah Legislature — Utah Code § 16-1a-212, Annual report for division (Effective 10/1/2026))

When is the Utah annual report due?

Each calendar year by the last day of the entity's anniversary month: the month its formation became effective with the Division or, for a foreign entity, the month its authority to conduct affairs in Utah took effect. It may be filed up to 60 days before that date. The Division may set a different period by rule. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “each calendar year on the last day of the anniversary month” (Utah Legislature — Utah Code § 16-1a-212, Annual report for division (Effective 10/1/2026))

How much does it cost to file in Utah?

The state's page we read does not state the fee. The state shows it when you file.

What happens if the Utah report is late?

The state's page we read does not state a late penalty. Ask the filing office before the date passes.
