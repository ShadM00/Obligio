STATE FILING · TENNESSEE

Tennessee annual report due date

Annual report (corporation): Due on or before the first day of the fourth month after the close of the corporation's fiscal year. If no fiscal-year close month was given in the charter, the Division of Business Services records December by default.

This page covers corporations. The Tennessee pages we read do not state when an LLC's annual report is due; check with the Secretary of State.

From the official Tennessee Secretary of State page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Who has to file the Tennessee Annual report (corporation)?

Corporations.

The official page says: “requires corporations to file an annual report with the Secretary of State” (Tennessee Secretary of State — Instructions, Charter For-Profit Corporation (SS-4417))

When is the Tennessee annual report due?

Due on or before the first day of the fourth month after the close of the corporation's fiscal year. If no fiscal-year close month was given in the charter, the Division of Business Services records December by default. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “on or before the first day of the fourth month following the end” (Tennessee Secretary of State — Instructions, Charter For-Profit Corporation (SS-4417))

How much does it cost to file in Tennessee?

$20, plus $20 more if the registered agent or registered office changes.

The official page says: “The annual report fee for a corporation is $20” (Tennessee Secretary of State — Business Services FAQs)

What happens if the Tennessee report is late?

A business entity that does not file its annual report on time may be administratively dissolved and placed in inactive status.

The official page says: “failed to file its annual report on a timely basis may be administratively dissolved” (Tennessee Secretary of State — Business Services FAQs)
