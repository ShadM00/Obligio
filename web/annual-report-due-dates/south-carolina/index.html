STATE FILING · SOUTH CAROLINA

South Carolina annual report due date

Annual report (Schedule D of the SC Corporate Tax return): Due with the corporate tax return, by the return due date: C corporations (SC1120) by April 15 for calendar-year filers, otherwise the 15th day of the 4th month after the tax year ends; S corporations (SC1120S) by March 15, otherwise the 15th day of the 3rd month after the tax year ends.

From the official South Carolina Department of Revenue page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Who has to file the South Carolina Annual report (Schedule D of the SC Corporate Tax return)?

Generally every domestic corporation, every foreign corporation qualified to do business in South Carolina, and any other corporation required to file an SC income tax return. Excluded: LLCs not taxed as corporations, corporations whose only SC contact is Secretary of State registration, tax-exempt nonprofits, banks, savings and loans, insurance companies and certain other bodies. Dormant corporations must still file.

The official page says: “every domestic corporation, every foreign corporation qualified to do business in South Carolina” (South Carolina Department of Revenue — Corporate FAQs)

When is the South Carolina annual report due?

Due with the corporate tax return, by the return due date: C corporations (SC1120) by April 15 for calendar-year filers, otherwise the 15th day of the 4th month after the tax year ends; S corporations (SC1120S) by March 15, otherwise the 15th day of the 3rd month after the tax year ends. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “License Fee due each year by the due date of your return” (South Carolina Department of Revenue — Corporate FAQs)

How much does it cost to file in South Carolina?

License Fee: 0.1% of capital stock and paid-in surplus plus $15, minimum $25 (corporations subject to the annual report).

The official page says: “License Fee rate is 0.1% of the corporation's capital stock and paid-in surplus plus $15.” (South Carolina Department of Revenue — Corporate FAQs)

What happens if the South Carolina report is late?

Late return: 5% of the unpaid tax per month, up to 25%. Late payment: 0.5% of the tax per month, up to 25%.

The official page says: “may be penalized 5% of the unpaid tax for each month the return is late” (South Carolina Department of Revenue — Corporate FAQs)
