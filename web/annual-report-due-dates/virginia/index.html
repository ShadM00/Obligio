STATE FILING · VIRGINIA

Virginia annual report due date

Annual report (Code of Virginia § 13.1-775): Each year on or before the last day of the month in which the corporation was incorporated or authorized in Virginia (stated as the last day of the twelfth month after that month, then the same date each year). It can be filed no earlier than three months before the due date. The State Corporation Commission can move a due date by 1 to 11 months.
Annual registration fee (Code of Virginia § 13.1-1062), payment only: Each year on or before the last day of the month in which the LLC was organized or registered in Virginia (stated as the last day of the twelfth month after that month, then the same date each year). Different dates apply to LLCs created by conversion or domestication. The Commission can move a due date by 1 to 11 months.

From the official Code of Virginia § 13.1-775 page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Annual report (Code of Virginia § 13.1-775)

Who files it

Domestic corporations and foreign corporations authorized to transact business in Virginia.

The official page says: “Each domestic corporation, and each foreign corporation authorized to transact business in the Commonwealth” (Code of Virginia § 13.1-775 — Annual report of domestic and foreign corporations (Virginia LIS))

When it is due

Each year on or before the last day of the month in which the corporation was incorporated or authorized in Virginia (stated as the last day of the twelfth month after that month, then the same date each year). It can be filed no earlier than three months before the due date. The State Corporation Commission can move a due date by 1 to 11 months. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “on or before the last day of the twelfth month next succeeding the month” (Code of Virginia § 13.1-775 — Annual report of domestic and foreign corporations (Virginia LIS))

What it costs

The state's page we read does not state the fee. The state shows it when you file.

If it is late

The state's page we read does not state a late penalty. Ask the filing office before the date passes.

Annual registration fee (Code of Virginia § 13.1-1062), payment only

Who files it

Domestic LLCs, protected series, and foreign LLCs and foreign protected series registered in Virginia.

The official page says: “Every domestic limited liability company, every protected series, every foreign limited liability company registered” (Code of Virginia § 13.1-775 — Annual report of domestic and foreign corporations (Virginia LIS))

When it is due

Each year on or before the last day of the month in which the LLC was organized or registered in Virginia (stated as the last day of the twelfth month after that month, then the same date each year). Different dates apply to LLCs created by conversion or domestication. The Commission can move a due date by 1 to 11 months. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “on or before the last day of the twelfth month next succeeding the month” (Code of Virginia § 13.1-775 — Annual report of domestic and foreign corporations (Virginia LIS))

What it costs

$50 per year.

The official page says: “by such date in each year thereafter, an annual registration fee of $50” (Code of Virginia § 13.1-775 — Annual report of domestic and foreign corporations (Virginia LIS))

If it is late

The state's page we read does not state a late penalty. Ask the filing office before the date passes.
