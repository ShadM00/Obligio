STATE FILING · KENTUCKY

Kentucky annual report due date

Annual Report: File between January 1 and June 30 each year; due by June 30.

From the official Kentucky Secretary of State page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Who has to file the Kentucky Annual Report?

All entities conducting business in Kentucky (domestic and foreign business entities).

The official page says: “all entities conducting business in the Commonwealth of Kentucky” (Kentucky Secretary of State — Annual Reports)

When is the Kentucky annual report due?

File between January 1 and June 30 each year; due by June 30. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “Reports can be filed any time between January 1 and June 30” (Kentucky Secretary of State — Annual Reports)

How much does it cost to file in Kentucky?

$15 filing fee.

The official page says: “accompanied by a $15 filing fee” (Kentucky Secretary of State — Annual Reports)

What happens if the Kentucky report is late?

Domestic entities that miss June 30 are administratively dissolved (inactive and in bad standing until they reinstate); foreign entities that do not file on time have their certificates of authority revoked.

The official page says: “Domestic entities that fail to file their annual reports by June 30 are administratively dissolved” (Kentucky Secretary of State — Annual Reports)

File online through the state's filing service.
