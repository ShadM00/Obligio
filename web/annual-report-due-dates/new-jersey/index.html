STATE FILING · NEW JERSEY

New Jersey annual report due date

Annual report: Due every year by the last day of the month in which the business was formed.

From the official Business.NJ.gov page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Who has to file the New Jersey Annual report?

Every business in New Jersey, including LLCs and corporations.

The official page says: “Every business in NJ must file an annual report.” (Business.NJ.gov — Taxes and Annual Report)

When is the New Jersey annual report due?

Due every year by the last day of the month in which the business was formed.

The official page says: “last day of the month, in the month in which you completed your business formation” (Business.NJ.gov — Taxes and Annual Report)

How much does it cost to file in New Jersey?

$75 filing fee.

The official page says: “submitting a $75 filing fee” (Business.NJ.gov — Taxes and Annual Report)

What happens if the New Jersey report is late?

Failure to file can result in revocation of the business. The business is responsible for filing even if no notice arrives from the State.

The official page says: “Failure to file can result in the revocation of your business.” (Business.NJ.gov — Taxes and Annual Report)
