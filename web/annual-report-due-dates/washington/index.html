STATE FILING · WASHINGTON

Washington annual report due date

Annual Report: Due by the last day of the month in which the business was originally formed or registered. It can be filed up to 180 days before that date, and filing early does not change the expiration date.

From the official Washington Secretary of State page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Who has to file the Washington Annual Report?

All domestic and foreign business entities.

The official page says: “All domestic and foreign business entities must file an Annual Report yearly” (Washington Secretary of State — Annual Reports)

When is the Washington annual report due?

Due by the last day of the month in which the business was originally formed or registered. It can be filed up to 180 days before that date, and filing early does not change the expiration date. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “due by the last day of the month in which the business was originally formed” (Washington Secretary of State — Annual Reports)

How much does it cost to file in Washington?

The state's page we read does not state the fee. The state shows it when you file.

What happens if the Washington report is late?

Not filing on or before the expiration date puts the business in delinquent status and may lead to administrative dissolution.

The official page says: “results in a delinquent status and may lead to administrative dissolution.” (Washington Secretary of State — Annual Reports)
