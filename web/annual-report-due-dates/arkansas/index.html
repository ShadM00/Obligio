STATE FILING · ARKANSAS

Arkansas annual report due date

Franchise Tax / Annual Report (Arkansas Corporate Franchise Tax Act of 1979): File on or before May 1 to avoid penalties.

From the official Arkansas Secretary of State page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Who has to file the Arkansas Franchise Tax / Annual Report (Arkansas Corporate Franchise Tax Act of 1979)?

Corporations, LLCs, banks and insurance companies registered in Arkansas.

The official page says: “requires all Corporations, LLC's, Banks, and Insurance Companies registered in Arkansas” (Arkansas Secretary of State — State Franchise Tax and Annual Report Filing System)

When is the Arkansas annual report due?

File on or before May 1 to avoid penalties. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “Filing due on or before May 1 to avoid penalties” (Arkansas Secretary of State — State Franchise Tax and Annual Report Filing System)

How much does it cost to file in Arkansas?

Varies by entity type: LLC or PLLC $150; corporations with stock minimum $150; corporations without stock $300; banks minimum $150; online card payments add a processing fee (page states $5.00 for credit card, $3 for electronic check).

The official page says: “Limited Liability Company LLC or PLLC N/A $150 $150” (Arkansas Secretary of State — State Franchise Tax and Annual Report Filing System)

What happens if the Arkansas report is late?

Failure to pay can bring additional fees, penalties and interest, or revocation of the authorization to do business; franchise taxes keep accruing even for revoked businesses; further BCS filings are prohibited for entities that fail to pay.

The official page says: “or even revocation of the authorization to do business” (Arkansas Secretary of State — State Franchise Tax and Annual Report Filing System)

File online through the state's filing service.
