STATE FILINGS · CORPORATIONS AND LLCS

Annual report due dates by state

Most states ask corporations and LLCs to file a short report every year or two to keep their records current, and many dissolve or penalise a business that misses it. The table gives each state's report and when it is due, taken from the state's own page and read on 20 November 2026. Open a state for who files it, the fee and the late penalty where the state states them, with the official wording quoted.

Organisational guidance, not legal or tax advice. Check the official page before you file. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Due rules as each state words them. Select a state for its sources.
StateReport and who filesWhen it is due
AlabamaAlabama Business Privilege Tax return (Form CPT) (c-corporations with a calendar year or a fiscal year other than June 30)Due no later than three and a half months after the start of the taxable year (for example, April 15 for a calendar-year C-corporation). The due date corresponds to the due date of the corresponding federal return.
Alabama Business Privilege Tax return (Form CPT) (c-corporations with a June 30 fiscal year)Due no later than two and a half months after the start of the taxable year (for example, September 15 for a June 30 fiscal-year C-corporation).
Alabama Business Privilege Tax return (Form PPT)
Limited liability entities		Due no later than two and a half months after the start of the taxable year (for example, March 15 for a calendar-year limited liability entity).
AlaskaBiennial Report (for-profit corporations, professional corporations, limited liability…)Due by January 2 of every even-numbered year if the entity first filed/registered in an even-numbered year, or of every odd-numbered year if it first filed in an odd-numbered year. The reporting period opens three months before the due date.
Biennial Report (nonprofit corporations, cooperatives and religious corporations)Due by July 2 of every even-numbered year if the entity first filed/registered in an even-numbered year, or of every odd-numbered year if it first filed in an odd-numbered year. The reporting period opens three months before the due date.
ArizonaAnnual Report (corporations)
Corporations (for-profit and nonprofit)		Each corporation files by its own designated due date every year; the due date is shown on the corporation's record on the ACC business website. The report can be filed online up to 90 days before it is due.
ArkansasFranchise Tax / Annual Report (Arkansas Corporate Franchise Tax Act of 1979)
Corporations, LLCs, banks and insurance companies registered in Arkansas		File on or before May 1 to avoid penalties.
CaliforniaStatement of Information (Statement of Information – CA and Out-of-State Corporation)
California stock corporations and qualified out-of-state (foreign) corporations		Every year, by the last day of the calendar month in which the corporation was originally incorporated or registered. The filing period is a six-month window ending that month (for example, a January registration files between August 1 and January 31). File an updated statement whenever the information changes.
Statement of Information (Statement of Information – CA and Out-of-State LLC)
California LLCs and qualified out-of-state (foreign) LLCs		Every two years, in odd or even years matching the year of initial registration, by the last day of the calendar month of registration. The filing period is a six-month window ending that month. File an updated statement whenever the information changes.
ColoradoPeriodic Report
Reporting entities, including limited liability companies, corporations,…		Each entity's 'Periodic report month' is shown on its Summary page in the business database. The report can be filed from two months before that month until two months after it without any penalty.
ConnecticutAnnual report (Limited Liability Company)
Limited liability companies (LLCs)		Due each year between January 1 and March 31 (since 2018; before 2018 LLC reports were due by the last day of the anniversary month).
Annual report (Corporation)
Corporations (stock and nonstock)		Due each year by the last day of the anniversary month of formation or registration; can be filed up to one month before the anniversary month.
DelawareAnnual Report and Franchise Tax (domestic corporations)
Active domestic (Delaware) corporations		On or before March 1 each year, covering the prior year; must be filed online.
Annual Report (foreign corporations)
Foreign corporations registered in Delaware		On or before June 30 each year.
Annual tax for LLCs, LPs and GPs (no annual report)
Domestic and foreign LLCs, limited partnerships and general partnerships…		Pay the annual tax for the prior year on or before June 1 each year.
District of ColumbiaBRA-25 Two-Year Biennial Report for Domestic and Foreign Filing Entities
Every domestic and foreign filing entity registered with the DLCP…		First report due by April 1 of the calendar year after the entity registers, then by April 1 every two years after that.
FloridaAnnual report (Fla. Stat. § 607.1622)
Domestic corporations and foreign corporations authorized to transact…		Between January 1 and May 1 each year. The first report is due between January 1 and May 1 of the year after the calendar year in which the articles of incorporation became effective or the foreign corporation got its certificate of authority.
Annual report (Fla. Stat. § 605.0212)
Florida LLCs and registered foreign LLCs		Between January 1 and May 1 each year. The first report is due between January 1 and May 1 of the year after the calendar year in which the articles of organization became effective or the foreign LLC got its certificate of authority.
GeorgiaAnnual Registration
Each business entity registered or filed with the Georgia Secretary of State…		Due by April 1 of each year it is due; can be filed as early as January 1. Registrations may be filed in advance for up to and including 3 calendar years.
HawaiiAnnual business report (annual report or annual statement)
Business entities registered with the DCCA Business Registration Division		Due by the last day of the calendar quarter in which the entity registered: registered Jan 1–Mar 31, due Mar 31; Apr 1–Jun 30, due Jun 30; Jul 1–Sep 30, due Sep 30; Oct 1–Dec 31, due Dec 31. An entity registered in the same year a report falls due does not file for that year.
KansasInformation Report (for-profit businesses on file with the Kansas Secretary of State)Due April 15 every two years: businesses formed in an even year file in each following even year; businesses formed in an odd year file in each following odd year.
Information Report (not-for-profit businesses on file with the Kansas Secretary of State)Due June 15 every two years, in even or odd years matching the year of formation.
KentuckyAnnual Report
All entities conducting business in Kentucky (domestic and foreign business…		File between January 1 and June 30 each year; due by June 30.
MaineAnnual report
Legal entities filed with the Maine Secretary of State (domestic and foreign…		Due by June 1 each year; file between January 1 and June 1. The first report is due in the calendar year after the entity was incorporated, formed or qualified.
MarylandAnnual Report (SDAT Form 1 — annual report/personal property return)
All entities that existed as of January 1 (including LLCs and corporations),…		Due by April 15 each year for every entity that existed on January 1. The first filing is due the year after the business is formed (e.g. formed any time in 2024, first Annual Report due by April 15, 2025).
MichiganAnnual Statement (Limited Liability Companies) / Annual Report and Annual Statement (Professional LLCs)
Limited liability companies and professional limited liability companies…		Due February 15 each year after organization/qualification; an LLC formed or qualified after September 30 does not file on the February 15 immediately following its formation. Online filing opens October 15.
Annual Report for Corporations
Profit corporations and professional corporations (nonprofit corporations…		Due May 15 each year after incorporation/qualification; online filing opens January 15.
MinnesotaAnnual renewal
Most business entities registered in Minnesota		By December 31 of each calendar year. The renewal can be filed at any time during the year it is due, and the entity's Business Record Details page shows its next renewal due date.
MississippiAnnual Report (for-profit corporations and limited liability companies operating in Mississippi)Can be filed any time on or after January 1 and is due by April 15 of that year. Must be filed online.
Annual Report (nonprofit corporations)Can be filed any time on or after January 1 and is due by May 15 of that year. Must be filed online.
MissouriAnnual registration report
All corporations doing business in Missouri, domestic and foreign		Each year by the end of the month in which the corporation incorporated or qualified (for corporations incorporated or qualified on or after July 1, 2003). A corporation that existed before July 1, 2003 is due at the end of the month shown on its last annual report. The due month cannot be changed.
MontanaAnnual report
LLCs and corporations (profit and nonprofit)		File each year. Reports filed January 1 to April 15 have no fee. Foreign entities must file by November 1 and domestic entities by December 1.
NebraskaBiennial occupation tax report
Domestic and foreign business corporations and professional corporations		Due by March 1 of even-numbered years; delinquent April 15.
Biennial report
Domestic and foreign LLCs and professional LLCs		Due by April 1 of odd-numbered years; delinquent June 16.
NevadaAnnual List of Officers, filed together with the State Business License renewal
Title 7 entities, whose State Business License renewal falls due with the…		On the last day of the anniversary month in which the license was originally filed. The Annual List and business license are filed together. The first Initial List and license are due by the last day of the month after formation.
New HampshireAnnual Report
Corporations, professional corporations, benefit corporations, professional…		Annual report and filing fee due by April 1 of each year following the year of registration.
New JerseyAnnual report
Every business in New Jersey, including LLCs and corporations		Due every year by the last day of the month in which the business was formed.
New MexicoCorporate Report (Corporate Reports Act, NMSA 1978 § 53-5-2) and supplemental report
Domestic and foreign corporations that are not exempted (exemptions listed…		First report within 30 days after the certificate of incorporation or certificate of authority is issued; then biennially, on or before the 15th day of the fourth month after the end of the corporation's taxable year. A supplemental report is due within 30 days of a change in registered office/agent, directors or officers, or principal place of business.
New YorkBiennial Statement
Domestic and foreign business corporations (Business Corporation Law § 408)…		Every two years, during the calendar month in which the original Certificate of Incorporation, Articles of Organization or Application for Authority was filed with the Department of State. Do not file before that month.
North CarolinaAnnual Report (Business Corporations and Banks)
Business corporations and banks		Due the 15th day of the fourth month after the entity's fiscal year ends (April 15 for a calendar-year entity).
Annual Report (Limited Liability Companies)
Limited liability companies (LLC or L3C)		Due April 15 of each year after the year the LLC was created.
North DakotaAnnual Report (domestic business corporations and domestic professional corporations)On or before August 1 each year, starting the year after the year of registration.
Annual Report (foreign business corporations and foreign professional corporations)On or before May 15 each year, starting the year after the year of registration.
Annual Report (limited liability companies (LLC) and professional limited liability…)On or before November 15 each year, starting the year after the year of registration.
Annual Report (farming or ranching LLCs and corporations, and authorized livestock farm…)On or before April 15 each year, starting the year after the year of registration.
Annual Report (limited partnerships, limited liability partnerships, limited liability…)On or before March 31 each year, starting the year after the year of registration.
OregonAnnual Report or Renewal
All business entity types except assumed business names (which renew every…		Due on the anniversary date of the original filing each year. The Secretary of State sends a renewal notice about 45 days before.
PennsylvaniaAnnual Report [DSCB:15-146] — corporations
Business and nonprofit corporations, domestic and foreign		File between January 1 and June 30 each year (deadline June 30).
Annual Report [DSCB:15-146] — limited liability companies
Limited liability companies, domestic and foreign		File between January 1 and September 30 each year (deadline September 30).
Rhode IslandAnnual Report
Limited liability companies, business corporations, and limited partnerships…		File between February 1 and May 1 each year, starting the calendar year after the business registers with the state.
Annual Report (domestic benefit corporations, Form 633)
Domestic benefit corporations		Within 120 days of the end of the corporation's fiscal year.
South CarolinaAnnual report (Schedule D of the SC Corporate Tax return)
Generally every domestic corporation, every foreign corporation qualified to…		Due with the corporate tax return, by the return due date: C corporations (SC1120) by April 15 for calendar-year filers, otherwise the 15th day of the 4th month after the tax year ends; S corporations (SC1120S) by March 15, otherwise the 15th day of the 3rd month after the tax year ends.
TennesseeAnnual report (corporation)
Corporations		Due on or before the first day of the fourth month after the close of the corporation's fiscal year. If no fiscal-year close month was given in the charter, the Division of Business Services records December by default.
TexasAnnual Franchise Tax Report, plus Public Information Report (Form 05-102) or Ownership Information Report (Form 05-167)
Each taxable entity formed or organized in Texas or doing business in Texas		Due May 15 each year; if May 15 falls on a weekend or holiday, due the next business day.
UtahAnnual report for division
Each domestic filing entity and registered foreign entity		Each calendar year by the last day of the entity's anniversary month: the month its formation became effective with the Division or, for a foreign entity, the month its authority to conduct affairs in Utah took effect. It may be filed up to 60 days before that date. The Division may set a different period by rule.
VermontAnnual Report (profit corporations and profit cooperative corporations)Within the first two and a half months following the fiscal year end on record (renewed each fiscal year).
Annual Report (limited liability companies (LLC/PLC/L3C))Within the first three months following the fiscal year end on record (renewed each fiscal year).
VirginiaAnnual report (Code of Virginia § 13.1-775)
Domestic corporations and foreign corporations authorized to transact…		Each year on or before the last day of the month in which the corporation was incorporated or authorized in Virginia (stated as the last day of the twelfth month after that month, then the same date each year). It can be filed no earlier than three months before the due date. The State Corporation Commission can move a due date by 1 to 11 months.
Annual registration fee (Code of Virginia § 13.1-1062), payment only
Domestic LLCs, protected series, and foreign LLCs and foreign protected…		Each year on or before the last day of the month in which the LLC was organized or registered in Virginia (stated as the last day of the twelfth month after that month, then the same date each year). Different dates apply to LLCs created by conversion or domestication. The Commission can move a due date by 1 to 11 months.
WashingtonAnnual Report
All domestic and foreign business entities		Due by the last day of the month in which the business was originally formed or registered. It can be filed up to 180 days before that date, and filing early does not change the expiration date.
West VirginiaAnnual Report
Organizations registered through the West Virginia Secretary of State (most…		File between January 1 and June 30 each year, starting the calendar year after the business registered with the Secretary of State.
WisconsinAnnual Report (domestic entities)
Domestic business corporations (Ch		Due each year after the calendar year of registration, during the calendar quarter containing the registration anniversary date; due by the last day of that quarter (March 31, June 30, September 30 or December 31).
Annual Report (foreign entities)
Foreign entities registered to transact business in Wisconsin		Due during the first calendar quarter (by March 31) of each year after the calendar year in which the foreign entity registered.
WyomingAnnual Report
Corporations, LLCs, LPs, RLLPs and SFs (statutory trusts have their own date)		Due on the first day of the anniversary month of the initial filing each year. For example, an entity first filed on May 15 is due May 1 each year. Statutory trust annual reports are due by January 1 each year.
