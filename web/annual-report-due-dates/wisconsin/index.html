STATE FILING · WISCONSIN

Wisconsin annual report due date

Annual Report (domestic entities): Due each year after the calendar year of registration, during the calendar quarter containing the registration anniversary date; due by the last day of that quarter (March 31, June 30, September 30 or December 31).
Annual Report (foreign entities): Due during the first calendar quarter (by March 31) of each year after the calendar year in which the foreign entity registered.

From the official Wisconsin Department of Financial Institutions page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Annual Report (domestic entities)

Who files it

Domestic business corporations (Ch. 180), nonstock corporations (Ch. 181), LLCs (Ch. 183), cooperatives (Ch. 185), LLPs (Ch. 178), LPs (Ch. 179, from 1/1/23), common law trusts (Ch. 226) and agricultural associations (Ch. 100.23).

The official page says: “Annual reports are required for the following entity types:” (Wisconsin Department of Financial Institutions — Business Entity FAQ)

When it is due

Due each year after the calendar year of registration, during the calendar quarter containing the registration anniversary date; due by the last day of that quarter (March 31, June 30, September 30 or December 31). It is filed every year.

The official page says: “during the calendar year quarter in which the anniversary date of the registration occurs” (Wisconsin Department of Financial Institutions — Business Entity FAQ)

What it costs

The state's page we read does not state the fee. The state shows it when you file.

If it is late

An entity that does not file goes delinquent; if not cured for an extended period it risks administrative dissolution. Entities that fail to file for several years are typically administratively dissolved.

The official page says: “the entity runs the risk of being administratively dissolved” (Wisconsin Department of Financial Institutions — Business Entity FAQ)

Annual Report (foreign entities)

Who files it

Foreign entities registered to transact business in Wisconsin.

The official page says: “A foreign entity registered to transact business in this state” (Wisconsin Department of Financial Institutions — Business Entity FAQ)

When it is due

Due during the first calendar quarter (by March 31) of each year after the calendar year in which the foreign entity registered. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “during the first calendar quarter of each year following the calendar year” (Wisconsin Department of Financial Institutions — Business Entity FAQ)

What it costs

The state's page we read does not state the fee. The state shows it when you file.

If it is late

Foreign entities that have not filed within four months of the March 31 due date may have their Certificate of Authority/Registration revoked or terminated.

The official page says: “within four months of the March 31st due date, are subject to having their Certificate” (Wisconsin Department of Financial Institutions — Business Entity FAQ)
