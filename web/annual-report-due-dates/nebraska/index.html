STATE FILING · NEBRASKA

Nebraska annual report due date

Biennial occupation tax report: Due by March 1 of even-numbered years; delinquent April 15.
Biennial report: Due by April 1 of odd-numbered years; delinquent June 16.

From the official Nebraska Secretary of State page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Biennial occupation tax report

Who files it

Domestic and foreign business corporations and professional corporations.

The official page says: “Domestic and Foreign Business Corporations and Professional Corporations biennial occupation tax reports” (Nebraska Secretary of State — Annual/Biennial Reporting)

When it is due

Due by March 1 of even-numbered years; delinquent April 15. It is filed every two years.

The official page says: “tax reports are due in the even numbered years by March 1, delinquent April 15” (Nebraska Secretary of State — Annual/Biennial Reporting)

What it costs

The state's page we read does not state the fee. The state shows it when you file.

If it is late

If the report is not filed by the delinquency date, the company is administratively dissolved or has its authority administratively revoked.

The official page says: “your company will be administratively dissolved or have its authority administratively revoked” (Nebraska Secretary of State — Annual/Biennial Reporting)

File online through the state's filing service.

Biennial report

Who files it

Domestic and foreign LLCs and professional LLCs.

The official page says: “Domestic and Foreign Limited Liability Companies and Professional Limited Liability Companies biennial reports” (Nebraska Secretary of State — Annual/Biennial Reporting)

When it is due

Due by April 1 of odd-numbered years; delinquent June 16. It is filed every two years.

The official page says: “biennial reports are due in the odd numbered years by April 1, delinquent June 16” (Nebraska Secretary of State — Annual/Biennial Reporting)

What it costs

The state's page we read does not state the fee. The state shows it when you file.

If it is late

If the report is not filed by the delinquency date, the company is administratively dissolved or has its authority administratively revoked.

The official page says: “your company will be administratively dissolved or have its authority administratively revoked” (Nebraska Secretary of State — Annual/Biennial Reporting)

File online through the state's filing service.
