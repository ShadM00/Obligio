STATE FILING · WEST VIRGINIA

West Virginia annual report due date

Annual Report: File between January 1 and June 30 each year, starting the calendar year after the business registered with the Secretary of State.

From the official West Virginia Secretary of State page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Who has to file the West Virginia Annual Report?

Organizations registered through the West Virginia Secretary of State (most company types). Requirements vary by company type.

The official page says: “requires organizations registered through the West Virginia Secretary of State to file an annual report” (West Virginia Secretary of State — Annual Reports)

When is the West Virginia annual report due?

File between January 1 and June 30 each year, starting the calendar year after the business registered with the Secretary of State. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “between January 1 and June 30 of each year following the calendar year” (West Virginia Secretary of State — Annual Reports)

How much does it cost to file in West Virginia?

$25.00 annual report filing fee.

The official page says: “pay the annual report $25.00 filing fee” (West Virginia Secretary of State — Annual Reports)

What happens if the West Virginia report is late?

Missing the June 30 deadline may result in monetary penalties. The amount is not stated. Administrative dissolution or revocation.

The official page says: “Failure to file by the June 30 deadline may result in monetary penalties” (West Virginia Secretary of State — Annual Reports)

File online through the state's filing service.
