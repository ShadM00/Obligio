STATE FILING · FLORIDA

Florida annual report due date

Annual report (Fla. Stat. § 607.1622): Between January 1 and May 1 each year. The first report is due between January 1 and May 1 of the year after the calendar year in which the articles of incorporation became effective or the foreign corporation got its certificate of authority.
Annual report (Fla. Stat. § 605.0212): Between January 1 and May 1 each year. The first report is due between January 1 and May 1 of the year after the calendar year in which the articles of organization became effective or the foreign LLC got its certificate of authority.

From the official Florida Senate page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Annual report (Fla. Stat. § 607.1622)

Who files it

Domestic corporations and foreign corporations authorized to transact business in Florida.

The official page says: “Each domestic corporation and each foreign corporation authorized to transact business in this state” (Florida Senate — 2026 Florida Statutes § 607.1622, Annual report for department)

When it is due

Between January 1 and May 1 each year. The first report is due between January 1 and May 1 of the year after the calendar year in which the articles of incorporation became effective or the foreign corporation got its certificate of authority. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “Subsequent annual reports must be delivered to the department between January 1 and May 1” (Florida Senate — 2026 Florida Statutes § 607.1622, Annual report for department)

What it costs

The state's page we read does not state the fee. The state shows it when you file.

If it is late

Until the report is filed and all fees and penalties are paid, the corporation cannot bring or maintain a lawsuit in a Florida court, and it can be dissolved or have its certificate of authority cancelled.

The official page says: “may not prosecute or maintain any action in any court of this state” (Florida Senate — 2026 Florida Statutes § 607.1622, Annual report for department)

Annual report (Fla. Stat. § 605.0212)

Who files it

Florida LLCs and registered foreign LLCs.

The official page says: “A limited liability company or a registered foreign limited liability company” (Florida Senate — 2026 Florida Statutes § 605.0212, Annual report for department (LLCs))

When it is due

Between January 1 and May 1 each year. The first report is due between January 1 and May 1 of the year after the calendar year in which the articles of organization became effective or the foreign LLC got its certificate of authority. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “Subsequent annual reports must be delivered to the department between January 1 and May 1” (Florida Senate — 2026 Florida Statutes § 607.1622, Annual report for department)

What it costs

The state's page we read does not state the fee. The state shows it when you file.

If it is late

Until the report is filed and all fees and penalties are paid, the LLC cannot maintain or defend a lawsuit in a Florida court, and it can be dissolved or have its certificate of authority cancelled.

The official page says: “may not maintain or defend any action in a court of this state” (Florida Senate — 2026 Florida Statutes § 605.0212, Annual report for department (LLCs))
