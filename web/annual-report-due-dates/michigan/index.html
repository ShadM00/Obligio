Michigan annual report due date

Annual Statement (Limited Liability Companies) / Annual Report and Annual Statement (Professional LLCs): Due February 15 each year after organization/qualification; an LLC formed or qualified after September 30 does not file on the February 15 immediately following its formation. Online filing opens October 15.
Annual Report for Corporations: Due May 15 each year after incorporation/qualification; online filing opens January 15.

From the official Michigan LARA Corporations Division page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Annual Statement (Limited Liability Companies) / Annual Report and Annual Statement (Professional LLCs)

Who files it

Limited liability companies and professional limited liability companies registered with the Corporations Division.

The official page says: “LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANIES and PROFESSIONAL LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANIES” (Michigan LARA Corporations Division — Annual Reports and Annual Statements)

When it is due

Due February 15 each year after organization/qualification; an LLC formed or qualified after September 30 does not file on the February 15 immediately following its formation. Online filing opens October 15. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “Due Date: February 15 each year after organization/qualification.” (Michigan LARA Corporations Division — Annual Reports and Annual Statements)

What it costs

LLC $25.00; PLLC $75.00.

The official page says: “LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANIES Fee: $25.00” (Michigan LARA Corporations Division — Annual Reports and Annual Statements)

If it is late

PLLC: $50.00 penalty fee if received after February 15 (no LLC late penalty stated). Not filing leads to dissolution, revocation or not-good-standing status after a two-year grace period.

The official page says: “Penalty fee of $50.00 if received after the February 15 due date.” (Michigan LARA Corporations Division — Annual Reports and Annual Statements)

File online through the state's filing service.

Annual Report for Corporations

Who files it

Profit corporations and professional corporations (nonprofit corporations also file, by October 1, $20 fee).

The official page says: “PROFIT CORPORATIONS and PROFESSIONAL CORPORATIONS” (Michigan LARA Corporations Division — Annual Reports and Annual Statements)

When it is due

Due May 15 each year after incorporation/qualification; online filing opens January 15. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “Due Date: May 15 each year after incorporation/qualification.” (Michigan LARA Corporations Division — Annual Reports and Annual Statements)

What it costs

Corporations $25.00 (nonprofit corporations $20.00).

The official page says: “CORPORATIONS Fee: $25.00 Penalties are imposed if reports” (Michigan LARA Corporations Division — Annual Reports and Annual Statements)

If it is late

Late penalty rises with lateness: May 16-31 $10; June $20; July $30; August $40; September 1 or later $50. Not filing leads to dissolution, revocation or not-good-standing status after a two-year grace period, or one year for foreign corporations.

The official page says: “Penalties are imposed if reports are not received by the May 15 due date.” (Michigan LARA Corporations Division — Annual Reports and Annual Statements)

File online through the state's filing service.
