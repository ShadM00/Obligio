STATE FILING · OREGON

Oregon annual report due date

Annual Report or Renewal: Due on the anniversary date of the original filing each year. The Secretary of State sends a renewal notice about 45 days before.

From the official Oregon Secretary of State page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Who has to file the Oregon Annual Report or Renewal?

All business entity types except assumed business names (which renew every two years).

The official page says: “All other business entity types renew every year.” (Oregon Secretary of State — Annual Report or Renewal)

When is the Oregon annual report due?

Due on the anniversary date of the original filing each year. The Secretary of State sends a renewal notice about 45 days before. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “Your renewal is due on the anniversary date of the original filing.” (Oregon Secretary of State — Annual Report or Renewal)

How much does it cost to file in Oregon?

The state's page we read does not state the fee. The state shows it when you file.

What happens if the Oregon report is late?

The state's page we read does not state a late penalty. Ask the filing office before the date passes.
