STATE FILING · MINNESOTA

Minnesota annual report due date

Annual renewal: By December 31 of each calendar year. The renewal can be filed at any time during the year it is due, and the entity's Business Record Details page shows its next renewal due date.

From the official Minnesota Secretary of State page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Who has to file the Minnesota Annual renewal?

Most business entities registered in Minnesota; the state's page does not list the entity types.

The official page says: “Most entities need to be renewed yearly to stay active in Minnesota.” (Minnesota Secretary of State — Renewals (Renewing your Business))

When is the Minnesota annual report due?

By December 31 of each calendar year. The renewal can be filed at any time during the year it is due, and the entity's Business Record Details page shows its next renewal due date. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “The deadline for renewals is December 31, but you can renew at any time” (Minnesota Secretary of State — Renewals (Renewing your Business))

How much does it cost to file in Minnesota?

Free for most entity types; reinstating after a missed renewal requires a fee.

The official page says: “annual renewals are free (for most entity types), while there is a fee to reinstate” (Minnesota Secretary of State — Renewals (Renewing your Business))

What happens if the Minnesota report is late?

The business is administratively dissolved, meaning no longer active in Minnesota, and must be reinstated by filing a current renewal and paying a fee. Its name is reserved for one year after dissolution.

The official page says: “If you miss the renewal, your business will be administratively dissolved” (Minnesota Secretary of State — Renewals (Renewing your Business))

File online through the state's filing service.
