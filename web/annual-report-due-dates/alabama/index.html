STATE FILING · ALABAMA

Alabama annual report due date

Alabama Business Privilege Tax return (Form CPT) (c-corporations with a calendar year or a fiscal year other than June 30): Due no later than three and a half months after the start of the taxable year (for example, April 15 for a calendar-year C-corporation). The due date corresponds to the due date of the corresponding federal return.
Alabama Business Privilege Tax return (Form CPT) (c-corporations with a June 30 fiscal year): Due no later than two and a half months after the start of the taxable year (for example, September 15 for a June 30 fiscal-year C-corporation).
Alabama Business Privilege Tax return (Form PPT): Due no later than two and a half months after the start of the taxable year (for example, March 15 for a calendar-year limited liability entity).

From the official Alabama Department of Revenue page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Alabama Business Privilege Tax return (Form CPT) (c-corporations with a calendar year or a fiscal year other than June 30)

Who files it

C-corporations with a calendar year or a fiscal year other than June 30.

The official page says: “For C-corporations with a calendar year or a fiscal year other than June 30” (Alabama Department of Revenue — When is the Alabama Business Privilege Tax return due?)

When it is due

Due no later than three and a half months after the start of the taxable year (for example, April 15 for a calendar-year C-corporation). The due date corresponds to the due date of the corresponding federal return.

The official page says: “no later than three and a half months after the beginning of a taxpayer’s” (Alabama Department of Revenue — When is the Alabama Business Privilege Tax return due?)

What it costs

The state's page we read does not state the fee. The state shows it when you file.

If it is late

The state's page we read does not state a late penalty. Ask the filing office before the date passes.
