STATE FILING · HAWAII

Hawaii annual report due date

Annual business report (annual report or annual statement): Due by the last day of the calendar quarter in which the entity registered: registered Jan 1–Mar 31, due Mar 31; Apr 1–Jun 30, due Jun 30; Jul 1–Sep 30, due Sep 30; Oct 1–Dec 31, due Dec 31. An entity registered in the same year a report falls due does not file for that year.

From the official Hawaii DCCA Business Registration Division page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Who has to file the Hawaii Annual business report (annual report or annual statement)?

Business entities registered with the DCCA Business Registration Division.

The official page says: “Submit annual reports for active business entities online” (Hawaii DCCA Business Registration Division — BREG Frequently Asked Questions)

When is the Hawaii annual report due?

Due by the last day of the calendar quarter in which the entity registered: registered Jan 1–Mar 31, due Mar 31; Apr 1–Jun 30, due Jun 30; Jul 1–Sep 30, due Sep 30; Oct 1–Dec 31, due Dec 31. An entity registered in the same year a report falls due does not file for that year. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “January 1 and March 31, the annual report or statement is due by March 31” (Hawaii DCCA Business Registration Division — BREG Frequently Asked Questions)

How much does it cost to file in Hawaii?

The state's page we read does not state the fee. The state shows it when you file.

What happens if the Hawaii report is late?

The state's page we read does not state a late penalty. Ask the filing office before the date passes.

File online through the state's filing service.
