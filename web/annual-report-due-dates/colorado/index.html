STATE FILING · COLORADO

Colorado annual report due date

Periodic Report: Each entity's 'Periodic report month' is shown on its Summary page in the business database. The report can be filed from two months before that month until two months after it without any penalty.

From the official Colorado Secretary of State page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Who has to file the Colorado Periodic Report?

Reporting entities, including limited liability companies, corporations, nonprofit corporations, foreign entities, cooperatives, limited partnerships formed after July 26, 2009, LLPs and LLLPs.

The official page says: “Reporting entities, such as limited liability companies, corporations, nonprofit corporations, and foreign entities” (Colorado Secretary of State — Business FAQs: Periodic reports)

When is the Colorado annual report due?

Each entity's 'Periodic report month' is shown on its Summary page in the business database. The report can be filed from two months before that month until two months after it without any penalty. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “filed two months prior to the Periodic Report month or two months after” (Colorado Secretary of State — Business FAQs: Periodic reports)

How much does it cost to file in Colorado?

$25.00 (online).

The official page says: “Periodic Report $25.00 n/a Periodic Report Late Filing Penalty $50.00” (Colorado Secretary of State — Business FAQs: Periodic reports)

What happens if the Colorado report is late?

$50.00 Periodic Report late filing penalty. The entity's status becomes 'Delinquent' if the report is not filed.

The official page says: “Periodic Report Late Filing Penalty $50.00” (Colorado Secretary of State — Business FAQs: Periodic reports)

File online through the state's filing service.
