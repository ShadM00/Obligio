STATE FILING · ALASKA

Alaska annual report due date

Biennial Report (for-profit corporations, professional corporations, limited liability…): Due by January 2 of every even-numbered year if the entity first filed/registered in an even-numbered year, or of every odd-numbered year if it first filed in an odd-numbered year. The reporting period opens three months before the due date.
Biennial Report (nonprofit corporations, cooperatives and religious corporations): Due by July 2 of every even-numbered year if the entity first filed/registered in an even-numbered year, or of every odd-numbered year if it first filed in an odd-numbered year. The reporting period opens three months before the due date.

From the official Alaska Division of Corporations, Business and Professional Licensing page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Biennial Report (for-profit corporations, professional corporations, limited liability…)

Who files it

For-profit corporations, professional corporations, limited liability companies and limited liability partnerships.

The official page says: “For Profit Corporations, Professional Corporations, Limited Liability Companies and Limited Liability Partnerships:” (Alaska Division of Corporations, Business and Professional Licensing — Biennial Reports)

When it is due

Due by January 2 of every even-numbered year if the entity first filed/registered in an even-numbered year, or of every odd-numbered year if it first filed in an odd-numbered year. The reporting period opens three months before the due date. It is filed every two years.

The official page says: “Biennial Reports are due by January 2nd, reports marked after February 1st” (Alaska Division of Corporations, Business and Professional Licensing — Biennial Reports)

What it costs

The state's page we read does not state the fee. The state shows it when you file.

If it is late

Late fee penalties apply to reports marked after February 1 (amount not stated on the state's page).

The official page says: “reports marked after February 1st incur late fee penalties” (Alaska Division of Corporations, Business and Professional Licensing — Biennial Reports)

File online through the state's filing service.

Biennial Report (nonprofit corporations, cooperatives and religious corporations)

Who files it

Nonprofit corporations, cooperatives and religious corporations.

The official page says: “Non Profit Corporations, Cooperatives and Religious Corporations:” (Alaska Division of Corporations, Business and Professional Licensing — Biennial Reports)

When it is due

Due by July 2 of every even-numbered year if the entity first filed/registered in an even-numbered year, or of every odd-numbered year if it first filed in an odd-numbered year. The reporting period opens three months before the due date. It is filed every two years.

The official page says: “Biennial Reports are due by July 2nd, reports post marked after August 1st” (Alaska Division of Corporations, Business and Professional Licensing — Biennial Reports)

What it costs

The state's page we read does not state the fee. The state shows it when you file.

If it is late

Late fee penalties apply to reports postmarked after August 1 (amount not stated on the state's page).

The official page says: “reports post marked after August 1st incur late fee penalties” (Alaska Division of Corporations, Business and Professional Licensing — Biennial Reports)

File online through the state's filing service.
