STATE FILING · DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA

District of Columbia annual report due date

BRA-25 Two-Year Biennial Report for Domestic and Foreign Filing Entities: First report due by April 1 of the calendar year after the entity registers, then by April 1 every two years after that.

From the official DC Department of Licensing and Consumer Protection page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Who has to file the District of Columbia BRA-25 Two-Year Biennial Report for Domestic and Foreign Filing Entities?

Every domestic and foreign filing entity registered with the DLCP Corporations Division (e.g., corporations, LLCs, partnerships).

The official page says: “Every entity registered with Corporations Divisions needs to file periodic BRA-25” (DC Department of Licensing and Consumer Protection — Corporations Division Business Registration FAQs)

When is the District of Columbia annual report due?

First report due by April 1 of the calendar year after the entity registers, then by April 1 every two years after that.

The official page says: “first Biennial Report is due by April 1 st the very next calendar year” (DC Department of Licensing and Consumer Protection — Corporations Division Business Registration FAQs)

How much does it cost to file in District of Columbia?

The state's page we read does not state the fee. The state shows it when you file.

What happens if the District of Columbia report is late?

Filing the biennial report, and keeping a registered agent, is required to stay in active and good standing status; the state's page does not set out a specific penalty.

The official page says: “in order to stay in active and good standing status” (DC Department of Licensing and Consumer Protection — Corporations Division Business Registration FAQs)

File online through the state's filing service.
