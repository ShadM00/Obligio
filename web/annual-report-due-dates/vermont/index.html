STATE FILING · VERMONT

Vermont annual report due date

Annual Report (profit corporations and profit cooperative corporations): Within the first two and a half months following the fiscal year end on record (renewed each fiscal year).
Annual Report (limited liability companies (LLC/PLC/L3C)): Within the first three months following the fiscal year end on record (renewed each fiscal year).

From the official Vermont Secretary of State page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Annual Report (profit corporations and profit cooperative corporations)

Who files it

Profit corporations and profit cooperative corporations.

The official page says: “As a profit corporation, profit cooperative corporation, or a limited liability company” (Vermont Secretary of State — Annual/Biennial Reports)

When it is due

Within the first two and a half months following the fiscal year end on record (renewed each fiscal year). It is filed every year.

The official page says: “within the first two and a half months (Profit Corps.)” (Vermont Secretary of State — Annual/Biennial Reports)

What it costs

The state's page we read does not state the fee. The state shows it when you file.

If it is late

The state's page we read does not state a late penalty. Ask the filing office before the date passes.

File online through the state's filing service.

Annual Report (limited liability companies (LLC/PLC/L3C))

Who files it

Limited liability companies (LLC/PLC/L3C).

The official page says: “As a profit corporation, profit cooperative corporation, or a limited liability company” (Vermont Secretary of State — Annual/Biennial Reports)

When it is due

Within the first three months following the fiscal year end on record (renewed each fiscal year). It is filed every year.

The official page says: “or three months (LLC/PLC/L3C) following the fiscal year end on record” (Vermont Secretary of State — Annual/Biennial Reports)

What it costs

The state's page we read does not state the fee. The state shows it when you file.

If it is late

The state's page we read does not state a late penalty. Ask the filing office before the date passes.

File online through the state's filing service.
