STATE FILING · MONTANA

Montana annual report due date

Annual report: File each year. Reports filed January 1 to April 15 have no fee. Foreign entities must file by November 1 and domestic entities by December 1.

From the official Montana Secretary of State page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Who has to file the Montana Annual report?

LLCs and corporations (profit and nonprofit).

The official page says: “Annual reports are required for all limited liability companies (LLCs) and corporations (profit and nonprofit).” (Montana Secretary of State — How do I file my annual report?)

When is the Montana annual report due?

File each year. Reports filed January 1 to April 15 have no fee. Foreign entities must file by November 1 and domestic entities by December 1. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “their annual report by November 1st, while domestic entitites must be filed by December 1st.” (Montana Secretary of State — How do I file my annual report?)

How much does it cost to file in Montana?

$35 if filed after April 15 (no fee for reports filed January 1 to April 15).

The official page says: “After April 15th, the fee is $35.” (Montana Secretary of State — How do I file my annual report?)

What happens if the Montana report is late?

The state's page we read does not state a late penalty. Ask the filing office before the date passes.

File online through the state's filing service.
