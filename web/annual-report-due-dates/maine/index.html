STATE FILING · MAINE

Maine annual report due date

Annual report: Due by June 1 each year; file between January 1 and June 1. The first report is due in the calendar year after the entity was incorporated, formed or qualified.

From the official Maine Secretary of State page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Who has to file the Maine Annual report?

Legal entities filed with the Maine Secretary of State (domestic and foreign business entities and nonprofit corporations).

The official page says: “your legal entity has been accepted for filing by the Secretary of State's office” (Maine Secretary of State — Filing Requirement Reminders)

When is the Maine annual report due?

Due by June 1 each year; file between January 1 and June 1. The first report is due in the calendar year after the entity was incorporated, formed or qualified. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “The first annual report filing must be filed between January 1st and June 1st” (Maine Secretary of State — Filing Requirement Reminders)

How much does it cost to file in Maine?

$85 for domestic business entities; $150 for foreign business entities ($35 for domestic and foreign nonprofit corporations).

The official page says: “fee is $85 for domestic business entities, $150 for foreign business entities” (Maine Secretary of State — Filing Requirement Reminders)

What happens if the Maine report is late?

A late filing penalty is assessed if the report is not received by the deadline (amount not stated). Not paying the late filing penalty results in administrative dissolution or revocation of the entity.

The official page says: “there will be a late filing penalty assessed” (Maine Secretary of State — Filing Requirement Reminders)

File online through the state's filing service.
