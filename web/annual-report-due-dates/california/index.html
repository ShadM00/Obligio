STATE FILING · CALIFORNIA

California annual report due date

Statement of Information (Statement of Information – CA and Out-of-State Corporation): Every year, by the last day of the calendar month in which the corporation was originally incorporated or registered. The filing period is a six-month window ending that month (for example, a January registration files between August 1 and January 31). File an updated statement whenever the information changes.
Statement of Information (Statement of Information – CA and Out-of-State LLC): Every two years, in odd or even years matching the year of initial registration, by the last day of the calendar month of registration. The filing period is a six-month window ending that month. File an updated statement whenever the information changes.

From the official California Secretary of State page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Statement of Information (Statement of Information – CA and Out-of-State Corporation)

Who files it

California stock corporations and qualified out-of-state (foreign) corporations; the FAQ also lists cooperative and credit union corporations as annual filers.

The official page says: “CA Stock Corporations and Qualified Out-of-State Corporations are required to file” (California Secretary of State — Business Entities FAQs)

When it is due

Every year, by the last day of the calendar month in which the corporation was originally incorporated or registered. The filing period is a six-month window ending that month (for example, a January registration files between August 1 and January 31). File an updated statement whenever the information changes.

The official page says: “yearly in the month of registration with the California Secretary of State” (California Secretary of State — Business Entities FAQs)

What it costs

$20 or $25; the state's page gives both amounts without saying which applies to which entity type.

The official page says: “The fee for filing a Statement of Information is $20 or $25.” (California Secretary of State — Business Entities FAQs)

If it is late

The Franchise Tax Board assesses a penalty if the statement is still not filed 60 days after the Secretary of State sends a notice of delinquency; the amount is not stated. The corporation's powers, rights and privileges (including the right to use its name in California) can be suspended or forfeited by the Secretary of State.

The official page says: “Penalties are assessed by the Franchise Tax Board when a business entity has not filed” (California Secretary of State — Business Entities FAQs)

File online through the state's filing service.

Statement of Information (Statement of Information – CA and Out-of-State LLC)

Who files it

California LLCs and qualified out-of-state (foreign) LLCs.

The official page says: “all California and qualified out-of-state limited liability companies” (California Secretary of State — Business Entities FAQs)

When it is due

Every two years, in odd or even years matching the year of initial registration, by the last day of the calendar month of registration. The filing period is a six-month window ending that month. File an updated statement whenever the information changes.

The official page says: “file every two years in the month of registration in even or odd years” (California Secretary of State — Business Entities FAQs)

What it costs

$20 or $25; the state's page gives both amounts without saying which applies to which entity type.

The official page says: “The fee for filing a Statement of Information is $20 or $25.” (California Secretary of State — Business Entities FAQs)

If it is late

The Franchise Tax Board assesses a penalty if the statement is still not filed 60 days after the Secretary of State sends a notice of delinquency; the amount is not stated. Suspension or forfeiture of the entity, in addition to Franchise Tax Board penalties.

The official page says: “Penalties are assessed by the Franchise Tax Board when a business entity has not filed” (California Secretary of State — Business Entities FAQs)

File online through the state's filing service.
