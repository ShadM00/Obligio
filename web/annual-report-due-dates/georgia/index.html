STATE FILING · GEORGIA

Georgia annual report due date

Annual Registration: Due by April 1 of each year it is due; can be filed as early as January 1. Registrations may be filed in advance for up to and including 3 calendar years.

From the official Georgia Secretary of State page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Who has to file the Georgia Annual Registration?

Each business entity registered or filed with the Georgia Secretary of State (corporations including profit, nonprofit and professional; LLCs; LPs, LLPs and LLLPs).

The official page says: “Each business entity registered or filed with the Office of Secretary of State” (Georgia Secretary of State — How to Guide: File Annual Registration)

When is the Georgia annual report due?

Due by April 1 of each year it is due; can be filed as early as January 1. Registrations may be filed in advance for up to and including 3 calendar years. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “Entity annual registrations are due by April 1st of each year it is due” (Georgia Secretary of State — How to Guide: File Annual Registration)

How much does it cost to file in Georgia?

$60 (profit corp., professional corp., benefit corp., LLC, LP, LLLP, trust estate); $40 (nonprofit corporation); $35 (foreign LLP); same online or paper.

The official page says: “Annual Registration (Profit Corp., Professional Corp., Benefit Corp., LLC, LP, LLLP, Trust Estate) | $60.00” (Georgia Secretary of State — How to Guide: File Annual Registration)

What happens if the Georgia report is late?

$25 penalty for late filing. Entities that do not timely file, with all required fees, may be administratively dissolved or have their authorization to transact business in Georgia revoked.

The official page says: “Penalty for Late Filing of Annual Registration | $25.00” (Georgia Secretary of State — How to Guide: File Annual Registration)
