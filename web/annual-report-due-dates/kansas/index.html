STATE FILING · KANSAS

Kansas annual report due date

Information Report (for-profit businesses on file with the Kansas Secretary of State): Due April 15 every two years: businesses formed in an even year file in each following even year; businesses formed in an odd year file in each following odd year.
Information Report (not-for-profit businesses on file with the Kansas Secretary of State): Due June 15 every two years, in even or odd years matching the year of formation.

From the official Kansas Secretary of State page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Information Report (for-profit businesses on file with the Kansas Secretary of State)

Who files it

For-profit businesses on file with the Kansas Secretary of State.

The official page says: “submitted every two years by any business on file with the Kansas Secretary of State” (Kansas Secretary of State — Information Reports)

When it is due

Due April 15 every two years: businesses formed in an even year file in each following even year; businesses formed in an odd year file in each following odd year.

The official page says: “for profit businesses are due by April 15th of their respective odd or even year” (Kansas Secretary of State — Information Reports)

What it costs

The state's page we read does not state the fee. The state shows it when you file.

If it is late

A three-month delinquency period after the due date, when the report can still be filed but the business is limited in what documents it can file. No dollar penalty is stated. If the three-month delinquency period passes, the business forfeits and cannot file any other document until it submits past-due reports and is reinstated.

The official page says: “This is a three-month interval after the due date” (Kansas Secretary of State — Information Reports)
