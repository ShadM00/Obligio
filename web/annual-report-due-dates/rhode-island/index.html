STATE FILING · RHODE ISLAND

Rhode Island annual report due date

Annual Report: File between February 1 and May 1 each year, starting the calendar year after the business registers with the state.
Annual Report (domestic benefit corporations, Form 633): Within 120 days of the end of the corporation's fiscal year.

From the official Rhode Island Department of State page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Annual Report

Who files it

Limited liability companies, business corporations, and limited partnerships or limited liability partnerships (Forms 632, 630, 634).

The official page says: “Limited Liability Companies, Business Corporations, Partnerships, and Benefit Corporations” (Rhode Island Department of State — File Your Annual Business Report)

When it is due

File between February 1 and May 1 each year, starting the calendar year after the business registers with the state. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “Limited Liability Company (LLC) February 1 – May 1 File Online Form 632” (Rhode Island Department of State — File Your Annual Business Report)

What it costs

$50 filing fee, plus a $2.50 Enhanced Access online fee when filing online.

The official page says: “Form 632 $50 $50 $2.50 $25 applied June 1” (Rhode Island Department of State — File Your Annual Business Report)

If it is late

$25 penalty applied June 1 (plus $3.00 to file online).

The official page says: “$25 applied June 1 (Plus $3.00 to file online)” (Rhode Island Department of State — File Your Annual Business Report)

File online through the state's filing service.

Annual Report (domestic benefit corporations, Form 633)

Who files it

Domestic benefit corporations.

The official page says: “Domestic Benefit Corporation Within 120 days of fiscal year end Form 633 $60” (Rhode Island Department of State — File Your Annual Business Report)

When it is due

Within 120 days of the end of the corporation's fiscal year. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “Domestic Benefit Corporation Within 120 days of fiscal year end Form 633 $60” (Rhode Island Department of State — File Your Annual Business Report)

What it costs

$60 filing fee.

The official page says: “Domestic Benefit Corporation Within 120 days of fiscal year end Form 633 $60” (Rhode Island Department of State — File Your Annual Business Report)

If it is late

$25 penalty applied 30 days after the end of the filing period.

The official page says: “$25 applied 30 days after end of filing period” (Rhode Island Department of State — File Your Annual Business Report)
