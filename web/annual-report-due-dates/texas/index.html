STATE FILING · TEXAS

Texas annual report due date

Annual Franchise Tax Report, plus Public Information Report (Form 05-102) or Ownership Information Report (Form 05-167): Due May 15 each year; if May 15 falls on a weekend or holiday, due the next business day.

From the official Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Who has to file the Texas Annual Franchise Tax Report, plus Public Information Report (Form 05-102) or Ownership Information Report (Form 05-167)?

Each taxable entity formed or organized in Texas or doing business in Texas. For report year 2024 and later, entities at or below the No Tax Due Threshold do not file a No Tax Due Report but must file a Public Information Report or Ownership Information Report (qualifying new veteran-owned businesses are exempt from both for their first five years).

The official page says: “imposed on each taxable entity formed or organized in Texas or doing business in Texas” (Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts — Franchise Tax)

When is the Texas annual report due?

Due May 15 each year; if May 15 falls on a weekend or holiday, due the next business day. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “falls on a weekend or holiday, the due date will be the next business day.” (Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts — Franchise Tax)

How much does it cost to file in Texas?

The state's page we read does not state the fee. The state shows it when you file.

What happens if the Texas report is late?

$50 penalty on each report filed after the due date; 5% penalty if tax is paid 1-30 days late, 10% if paid more than 30 days late; interest on past-due tax starts 61 days after the due date. Entities that fail to meet franchise tax filing requirements may receive Form 05-211, Texas Notice of Intent to Forfeit Right to Transact Business, or Form 05-213, Texas Notice of Forfeiture of Registration.

The official page says: “A $50 penalty is assessed on each report filed after the due date.” (Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts — Franchise Tax)

File online through the state's filing service.
