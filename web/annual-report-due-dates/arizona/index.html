STATE FILING · ARIZONA

Arizona annual report due date

Annual Report (corporations): Each corporation files by its own designated due date every year; the due date is shown on the corporation's record on the ACC business website. The report can be filed online up to 90 days before it is due.

From the official Arizona Corporation Commission page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Who has to file the Arizona Annual Report (corporations)?

Corporations (for-profit and nonprofit). Arizona LLCs are not required to file annual reports.

The official page says: “Only corporations are required to file annual reports.” (Arizona Corporation Commission — Business Services FAQs)

When is the Arizona annual report due?

Each corporation files by its own designated due date every year; the due date is shown on the corporation's record on the ACC business website. The report can be filed online up to 90 days before it is due.

The official page says: “Each corporation must file their annual report by their designated due date each year.” (Arizona Corporation Commission — Business Services FAQs)

How much does it cost to file in Arizona?

$45 for for-profit corporations; $10 for nonprofit corporations.

The official page says: “fee of $45 and nonprofit corporations have an annual report filing fee of $10” (Arizona Corporation Commission — Business Services FAQs)

What happens if the Arizona report is late?

For-profit corporations: $9.00 per month accrues once the deadline is missed. Nonprofit corporations are not assessed penalties. A delinquency notice is sent and status changes to Pending Inactive; about 60 days later a final delinquency notice; about 60 days after that the corporation is administratively dissolved. Reinstatement is possible for six years from dissolution at a cost of $100.

The official page says: “penalties in the amount of $9.00 per month begin to accrue” (Arizona Corporation Commission — Business Services FAQs)
