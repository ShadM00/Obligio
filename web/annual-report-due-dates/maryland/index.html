STATE FILING · MARYLAND

Maryland annual report due date

Annual Report (SDAT Form 1 — annual report/personal property return): Due by April 15 each year for every entity that existed on January 1. The first filing is due the year after the business is formed (e.g. formed any time in 2024, first Annual Report due by April 15, 2025).

From the official Maryland Business Express page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Who has to file the Maryland Annual Report (SDAT Form 1 — annual report/personal property return)?

All entities that existed as of January 1 (including LLCs and corporations), whether or not they own property or did business.

The official page says: “All entities are required to file a Form 1” (Maryland Business Express — Maintain Good Standing Status)

When is the Maryland annual report due?

Due by April 15 each year for every entity that existed on January 1. The first filing is due the year after the business is formed (e.g. formed any time in 2024, first Annual Report due by April 15, 2025). It is filed every year.

The official page says: “by April 15th if they existed as of January 1st.” (Maryland Business Express — Maintain Good Standing Status)

How much does it cost to file in Maryland?

The state's page we read does not state the fee. The state shows it when you file.

What happens if the Maryland report is late?

A late filing penalty applies to a late Form 1 (amount not stated on the state's page). A business not in Good Standing that does not fix it is forfeited; a forfeited business cannot legally operate in Maryland. Causes of forfeiture include failing to file a previous-year Form 1 (domestic) or current-year Form 1 (foreign), or not paying a late filing penalty.

The official page says: “pay an outstanding late filing penalty resulting from the late filing of a Form 1” (Maryland Business Express — Maintain Good Standing Status)

File online through the state's filing service.
