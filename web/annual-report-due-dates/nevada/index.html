STATE FILING · NEVADA

Nevada annual report due date

Annual List of Officers, filed together with the State Business License renewal: On the last day of the anniversary month in which the license was originally filed. The Annual List and business license are filed together. The first Initial List and license are due by the last day of the month after formation.

From the official Nevada Secretary of State page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Who has to file the Nevada Annual List of Officers, filed together with the State Business License renewal?

Title 7 entities, whose State Business License renewal falls due with the Annual List of Officers (the state's page does not list which entity types are Title 7). Sole proprietors and partnerships renew the license by the expiration date on it instead.

The official page says: “For Title 7 entities, the renewal date coincides with the due date” (Nevada Secretary of State — State Business License - FAQ)

When is the Nevada annual report due?

On the last day of the anniversary month in which the license was originally filed. The Annual List and business license are filed together. The first Initial List and license are due by the last day of the month after formation. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “on the last day of the anniversary month in which the license was originally filed” (Nevada Secretary of State — State Business License - FAQ)

How much does it cost to file in Nevada?

State Business License renewal: $500 for corporations, $200 for all other entity types. The Annual List of Officers fee is not stated on the state's page.

The official page says: “$500 for Corporations, and $200 for all other business entity types” (Nevada Secretary of State — State Business License - FAQ)

What happens if the Nevada report is late?

$100 penalty in addition to the annual business license fee if not paid by the due date. The entity goes into default. After one year in default it is revoked, and reinstatement requires paying all back fees and penalties, including the business license fee.

The official page says: “shall pay a penalty in the amount of $100 in addition to the annual fee” (Nevada Secretary of State — State Business License - FAQ)

File online through the state's filing service.
