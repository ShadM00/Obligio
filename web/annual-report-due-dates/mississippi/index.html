STATE FILING · MISSISSIPPI

Mississippi annual report due date

Annual Report (for-profit corporations and limited liability companies operating in Mississippi): Can be filed any time on or after January 1 and is due by April 15 of that year. Must be filed online.
Annual Report (nonprofit corporations): Can be filed any time on or after January 1 and is due by May 15 of that year. Must be filed online.

From the official Mississippi Secretary of State page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Annual Report (for-profit corporations and limited liability companies operating in Mississippi)

Who files it

For-profit corporations and limited liability companies operating in Mississippi.

The official page says: “The Annual Report for profit corporations and limited liability companies” (Mississippi Secretary of State — Annual Reports)

When it is due

Can be filed any time on or after January 1 and is due by April 15 of that year. Must be filed online. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “on or after January 1st of each year and are due by April 15th” (Mississippi Secretary of State — Annual Reports)

What it costs

The state's page we read does not state the fee. The state shows it when you file.

If it is late

Failure to file may result in administrative dissolution; the state's page states corporations that fail to file will be administratively dissolved.

The official page says: “Failure to file the Annual Report may result in the administrative dissolution of the organization.” (Mississippi Secretary of State — Annual Reports)

File online through the state's filing service.

Annual Report (nonprofit corporations)

The official page says: “on or after January 1st of each year and are due by May 15th” (Mississippi Secretary of State — Annual Reports)

File online through the state's filing service.
