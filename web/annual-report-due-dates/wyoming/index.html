STATE FILING · WYOMING

Wyoming annual report due date

Annual Report: Due on the first day of the anniversary month of the initial filing each year. For example, an entity first filed on May 15 is due May 1 each year. Statutory trust annual reports are due by January 1 each year.

From the official Wyoming Secretary of State page, read on 20 November 2026. Your own date depends on when and how your business was formed.

Who has to file the Wyoming Annual Report?

Corporations, LLCs, LPs, RLLPs and SFs (statutory trusts have their own date).

The official page says: “Annual Reports for corporations, LLCs, LPs, RLLPs and SFs are due” (Wyoming Secretary of State — Annual Report Instructions (wyobiz.wyo.gov))

When is the Wyoming annual report due?

Due on the first day of the anniversary month of the initial filing each year. For example, an entity first filed on May 15 is due May 1 each year. Statutory trust annual reports are due by January 1 each year. It is filed every year.

The official page says: “due on the first day of the anniversary month of formation.” (Wyoming Secretary of State — Annual Report Instructions (wyobiz.wyo.gov))

How much does it cost to file in Wyoming?

The license tax amount is not stated on the state's page. Filing online adds a payment card processing fee of 2.4% (minimum $1) of the filing fee. E-filing is not allowed if the annual report fee is over $500.

The official page says: “payment card processing fee of 2.4% (minimum $1) of the filing fee.” (Wyoming Secretary of State — Annual Report Instructions (wyobiz.wyo.gov))

What happens if the Wyoming report is late?

The state's page we read does not state a late penalty. Ask the filing office before the date passes.

File online through the state's filing service.
