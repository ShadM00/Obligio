Common questions

Reminders are not arriving

Reminders need notification permission for Obligio. In the app, open Settings and choose Notification preferences to turn them on, then use Send test reminder: a test arrives about a minute later. Obligio reminds you 30, 14, 7 and 1 days before each due date. If the test does not arrive, check that notifications for Obligio are allowed in your phone's settings.

How many obligations can I track for free?

Three. Obligio Plus adds unlimited obligations, recurring obligations that roll forward when completed, and documents attached to each obligation. Prices are shown in the app.

I bought Obligio Plus on another phone

Sign in with the same account, open the Obligio Plus screen and choose Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by the App Store or Google Play, so cancel in your Apple ID subscriptions or in the Google Play Store under Payments and subscriptions. Deleting your Obligio account does not cancel it.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, open Settings and choose Delete account. It permanently deletes your businesses, every obligation you track and every document you have attached, and it cannot be undone. Our privacy policy describes what is stored.

Where do I find my state's filing deadline?

Our table of annual report due dates by state quotes each state's own page with the date we read it. The state's page is the authority for your business.
