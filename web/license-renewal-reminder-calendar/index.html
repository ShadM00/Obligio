License renewal reminders in Google Calendar: the setup that works, where it stops, and when a tracker fits better

Google Calendar works as a license renewal reminder calendar if you give each license two events, not one. The first is an all-day event on the expiry date, repeating on the license's cycle (every year, every two years), with reminders 4 weeks, 2 weeks, 1 week and 1 day before. The second is a "start the renewal" event 60 to 90 days earlier, because Google allows no reminder more than 4 weeks before an event and no more than five per event. That is enough for a handful of licenses. A calendar does not know whether you renewed. The event repeats either way, and a missed date simply scrolls into the past. Once you hold more than about five dated items, or need the certificate kept beside the date, a tracker does that job better.

Set it up in Google Calendar: two events per license

These steps use Google Calendar on a computer, following Google's own help pages for recurring events and notifications.

Event 1: the expiry date

  1. Click Create. Title it with the license and number, for example "CSLB license 1234567 expires".
  2. Tick All day and set the date to the expiry date printed on the license.
  3. Next to "Does not repeat", open the menu and choose the license's cycle. For a two-year license, choose Custom and set it to repeat every 2 years.
  4. Under notifications, click Add notification until you have four: 4 weeks, 2 weeks, 1 week and 1 day before, each at a time you will be at work.
  5. In the description, paste the board's renewal page link and the license number. Click Save.

Event 2: start the renewal

  1. Create a second all-day event 60 to 90 days before the expiry date, titled "Start renewal: CSLB 1234567".
  2. Repeat it on the same cycle as event 1.
  3. Give it a reminder 1 day before, and list in the description what the renewal needs: fee, continuing education, bond, insurance certificate.

Ninety days is the start we suggest in our contractor license renewal guide, because continuing-education hours and insurance certificates take weeks to arrange. If your board only needs a fee, 60 days is enough.

Worked example: a California contractor license expiring 31 January 2027

The California Contractors State License Board (CSLB) renews an active license every two years and sends the renewal application about 60 days before expiry; if none has arrived 45 days before, you can order one (CSLB, General renewal information). Here is how each reminder you would want maps onto Google Calendar:

If you renew on 10 December 2026, nothing in the calendar changes. The reminders on 3, 17, 24 and 30 January still fire, and you dismiss them. That is harmless. The risk runs the other way: if you never renew, the calendar shows 31 January 2027 like any other past day, and the next event appears in 2029 as if all were well.

Calendar or tracker: a decision rule

  • Up to about five licenses and policies, and you open your calendar every working day: use Google Calendar with the two-event setup. It costs nothing and you already look at it.
  • More than five, or dates on different cycles (annual insurance, two-year license, five-year permit): keep a list with a status column, either the spreadsheet set up this way or a tracker app. The calendar can still carry the expiry events.
  • Someone else needs to see what is overdue, or an inspector or general contractor asks for proof: use a tracker that keeps the document with the date.

Obligio, our app for iPhone and Android, is one such tracker. It reminds you 30, 14, 7 and 1 days before each date, at 9 am. That schedule is fixed, so it cannot give a 90-day warning either; add a separate "start renewal" item for that. A date that passes without being marked done shows as overdue. With Plus, recurring items open their next cycle only when you mark the current one done, and you can attach the certificate. It does not sync to Google Calendar, does not share with a second person, and does not check any board.

If you import a calendar file instead

Our renewal reminder calculator makes a standard .ics calendar file with the reminders built in. After importing it into Google Calendar, open the event and check that the notifications are there. Google's import help page does not say whether imported reminders are kept, and we have not tested every account type. Add them by hand if they are missing.

Questions

How far ahead can Google Calendar remind me about a license renewal?

Google's Calendar API reference allows reminders between 0 and 40,320 minutes (4 weeks) before an event, and at most five custom reminders per event. For a 60- or 90-day warning, create a second event on that date called "Start the renewal" and give it its own reminders.

If I share the calendar with my bookkeeper, will they get my reminders?

No. Google says notification settings are personal and apply only to your account, even for people you share the calendar with. Each person has to set their own notifications.

What should a repeating renewal event be based on: the expiry date or the day I renewed?

The expiry date printed on the license. When the renewed license arrives, check that the next event in the series matches the new expiry date. If it does not, edit that event and choose "This and following events" so the rest of the series moves with it.
