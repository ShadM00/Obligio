Renewal reminder calculator

Enter the date a license, permit, insurance policy or state filing is due and this lists the days to be reminded: 30, 14, 7 and 1 days before by default, the same schedule as the Obligio app. It also makes a calendar file with those reminders built in. It runs in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Whole days, separated by commas. Up to 730.

How it works

Reminder date = due date − reminder days, counted in calendar days. The calendar file is a standard .ics event on the due date with one alert per reminder, so Apple Calendar, Google Calendar and Outlook show the alerts without any app. If the date repeats, the file repeats it every year or every two years; check each new date with the issuing authority, because rules and dates change.

Questions

How are the reminder dates worked out?

Each reminder is the due date minus that many calendar days. A reminder that lands on a weekend is marked, not moved, because rules on weekends and holidays differ by authority.

Does this store what I type?

No. The dates are worked out in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere. The calendar file is made on your device.

Which reminder days should I use?

The defaults, 30, 14, 7 and 1 days before, are the ones the Obligio app uses. Change them to any whole numbers of days up to 730.
