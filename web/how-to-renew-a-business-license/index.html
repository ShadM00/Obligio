How to renew a business license: the six steps, and what to have ready

To renew a business license, find the agency named on the license, renew through that agency's own portal (or by mail or in person) inside its renewal window, and send everything the renewal asks for besides the fee: updated details, signatures, and any certificate it needs, such as proof of workers' compensation. Then check that the status has changed to current and file the new certificate. Every license renews separately with the agency that issued it, so a city license, a state license and a trade board license are three renewals, not one. Below are the six steps, what each agency in the examples actually asks for, a rule for how early to start, and a checklist to print. The examples are American; Australians spell it licence, and the ASIC annual review guide covers Australian companies.

Step 1: Find out who issued each license

The issuing agency's name is printed on the license. Write it down for each one, because there is rarely a single place that renews everything. The SBA says you "may also need licenses from your county or city" as well as the state, and that "you'll have to research your own state, county, and city regulations" (SBA, Apply for licenses and permits). New York's state portal says the same about renewals: many licenses "can be renewed online through the issuing agency's website" (NY Business Express).

Step 2: Find the window and the deadline

Two dates matter: the first day the agency will accept the renewal, and the expiry date. They differ by agency:

  • Washington: "You are eligible to renew your Business License up to two months before the expiration date printed on your license." Renewals received after that date are charged a late penalty (WA DOR).
  • California contractors: the board sends a renewal application about 60 days before expiry. If it has not arrived 45 days before, order one online or on 1-800-321-2752 (CSLB).
  • Columbia, SC: one date for every license, April 30. Renewal forms are mailed at the start of the year. After April 30 a 5% penalty is added, and another 5% on the first of each month until paid (Columbia).

Not getting the notice does not move the date. Columbia's page says "failure to receive notice shall not constitute a defense", and New York's says that you may be reminded "but you are responsible for staying in compliance."

Step 3: Gather what the renewal asks for besides the fee

This is the step that causes late renewals. The fee takes a minute; a missing certificate or signature can take weeks. What the example agencies ask for:

  • Your login and license number. NY Business Express notes that "some agencies require you to set up an online account for renewals". Set it up before the window opens, not on the last day.
  • Current details. Address, owners and officers. The CSLB asks for the new address if it changed, and a street address if you use a PO box. Columbia requires a name or address change to be reported within ten days, not at renewal.
  • Proof of insurance or an exemption. A California contractor with employees attaches a current certificate of workers' compensation insurance; one without employees certifies they "continue to have no employees". Without it, "the license will not be renewed" (CSLB, Completing your renewal application).
  • Every required signature. For a California contractor that means the owner, or all general partners, or the listed officers, and every qualifier (same page).
  • Re-validation forms. In Columbia, a home occupation or mobile food business resubmits its home occupation form or food service questionnaire with the renewal, so zoning can check it again.
  • Continuing education, a bond or an inspection, where the license requires them. Check your agency's renewal page for these words.

Step 4: Submit it, by the route that is fastest for you

Online is usually quickest. Columbia gives its own processing times: portal renewals up to 3 to 5 days, in person immediate, mail 10 business days, and up to 8 weeks for mail in the February to May peak. Card payments in its portal carry a 2.90% fee and ACH $1.25. Washington takes renewals online through My DOR or by a paper form requested on 360-705-6705, and recommends mailing early "as postmarks may take a few days". The CSLB accepts a personal, certified or cashier's check or money order, never cash. Its timely active renewal fee is $450 for a sole owner and $700 for other owners. After expiry those become $675 and $1,050 (CSLB fees).

Step 5: Confirm it went through

Paying is not the same as being renewed. The CSLB warns that "incomplete applications will be returned for corrections and may result in a period of unlicensed time." A week after submitting, look up the license on the agency's public lookup (Washington's Business Lookup, CSLB License Check) and check the new expiry date. Save a screenshot. When the certificate arrives, store it, and post it if your license must be displayed: Washington notes that some licenses must be posted before you open to the public.

Step 6: Set the next renewal before you close the file

You will never know the next date better than today. Add it to your calendar or tracker with three reminders: the day the window opens, 30 days before expiry and 7 days before. The renewal tracker guide explains the reasoning and has a template to copy.

How early to start: a rule

  • The renewal is a fee and a form in a portal you can log into: start when the window opens, or 30 days before expiry if there is no window.
  • It needs something from someone else (an insurance certificate, a bond, continuing education hours, a signature from a partner or officer, an inspection): start 90 days before expiry, because you are waiting on them.
  • You are renewing by mail, or the agency renews everyone on one date (like Columbia's April 30): start as soon as the form arrives. Peak-season mail took up to 8 weeks there.

When this page does not apply

  • The license has already expired. The consequences and the fix-it order are in what happens if a business license expires. Renew today if the portal lets you.
  • You sold the business or changed its structure. A renewal may not be the right form. Columbia, for example, says a license "shall not be transferable" and a change of ownership needs a new license. Ask the agency.
  • You need to know which licenses you need at all. Use your state's business portal or licensing wizard (Washington's Business Licensing Wizard, New York's Business Wizard), or the SBA's local assistance.
  • It is a tax filing or annual report rather than a license. See the small business compliance calendar.
  • Your case turns on a notice from the agency or a legal question. Phone the agency, or ask a licensed attorney or accountant. This page is organizational guidance, not legal or tax advice.
  • You hold a state contractor license. Board cycles, late fees and a reminder plan that covers the bond and insurance are in contractor license renewal dates and reminders.

Questions

How do I renew my business license online?

Go to the website of the agency printed on the license, not a third-party site, log in to its portal (set up the account first if it asks for one), find the renewal in your account, update your details, attach any certificate it asks for, and pay. Washington renews through My DOR; Columbia, South Carolina, through its self-service portal or the statewide renewal portal. Then check the license lookup a week later to confirm the new expiry date.

How early can I renew a business license?

It depends on the agency. Washington accepts a renewal up to two months before the printed expiry date. The California Contractors State License Board mails the renewal application about 60 days before expiry. Columbia, South Carolina, renews every license on one date, April 30, and mails forms at the start of the year. If your agency does not say, start 30 days before expiry, or 90 days if the renewal needs an insurance certificate, bond, training or someone else's signature.

Do I renew city, county and state licenses separately?

Usually, yes. Each license renews with the agency that issued it. The SBA says you may need licenses from your county or city as well as the state. Some states bundle city endorsements into one renewal: Washington's Business Licensing Service renews state and city endorsements together on one license. Check whether each of yours is covered by a bundle before assuming it is.

What if I did not get a renewal notice?

Renew anyway; the date on the license is the deadline. Columbia, South Carolina, says failure to receive notice is no defense and does not waive penalties. The California Contractors State License Board asks you to order a renewal application if it has not arrived 45 days before expiry. Update your mailing address with the agency at the same time.
