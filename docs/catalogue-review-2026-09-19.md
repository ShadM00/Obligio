# Catalogue source check, 19 September 2026

Every catalogue entry read against its cited source, and where that source was
silent or unreachable, against the official page or statute that does state the
rule. This was a machine-assisted check by Claude, not the attestation that
`reviewedAt` records: nothing was seeded, edited or marked reviewed because of
it. Use it as the worklist for the human review.

- International rules (live in production since 18 Sep): 3 minor, 6 match
- US state reports (drafts, not seeded): 3 discrepancy, 6 unverifiable, 29 minor, 13 match — includes New Mexico, still absent
- US federal industry (drafts, not seeded): 1 discrepancy, 4 minor, 2 match

Verdicts: **Match** — the source read supports every claim. **Minor** — correct
as far as it goes, but a claim rests on a page other than the cited one, or a
penalty or consequence the source states is missing. **Discrepancy** — wrong or
dead source, or a claim the rules contradict. **Unverifiable** — the official
site blocked every automated reader; search excerpts of it were consistent, but
someone should read it in a browser. Sites behind bot checks were not bypassed.

Most "minor" entries share one fix: the drafting rule keeps late penalties
where the source states them, and many drafts left them out.
