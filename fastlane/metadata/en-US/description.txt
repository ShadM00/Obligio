Obligio helps small-business owners keep every license, permit, insurance renewal and filing deadline in one place.

Add each obligation once, set its renewal date, and Obligio reminds you 30, 14, 7 and 1 days before it falls due — enough notice to gather the paperwork, and a final catch if you left it late.

TRACK WHAT MATTERS
Business and trade licenses, local and state permits, insurance renewals, tax filing deadlines, health and safety inspections, staff certifications, and any recurring document with an expiry date.

SUGGESTED FOR YOUR STATE AND INDUSTRY
Tell Obligio where you operate and what you do, and it suggests obligations that commonly apply — each linked to the authority it came from, with the date it was last reviewed. You pick which to track and set your own dates, because the real deadline depends on your entity type and filing history.

KEEP THE PROOF WITH THE DEADLINE
Attach the certificate, policy or permit to the obligation it proves, so it is where you expect it when an inspector asks.

COMPLIANCE HEALTH AT A GLANCE
One view of what is current, upcoming and overdue.

FREE AND PLUS
Free tracks up to three obligations with full reminders. Obligio Plus adds unlimited obligations, document evidence, and recurring obligations that roll forward automatically when you mark one done.

PRIVACY
No analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting. We do not track you across other apps and we do not sell personal information.

Obligio provides organizational tools, not legal or tax advice. It does not file anything on your behalf. Requirements vary and change — always confirm your own deadlines with the relevant authority.
