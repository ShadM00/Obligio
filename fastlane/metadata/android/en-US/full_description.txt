Track every business license, permit, insurance renewal and filing deadline in one place — and never lose a business license to a date you forgot.

Obligio is a compliance calendar for small business owners. Add each obligation once, set its renewal date, and get reminded 30, 14, 7 and 1 days before it falls due. Attach the certificate or permit as evidence so the proof sits with the deadline it belongs to.

WHY OWNERS USE OBLIGIO

A missed license renewal can stop a business trading. A late filing adds penalties. An expired insurance certificate can void a contract on the day you need it most. These deadlines rarely arrive together, which is exactly why they get missed.

Obligio keeps them in one calendar, sorted by what needs attention next.

WHAT YOU CAN TRACK

• Business licenses and trade licenses
• Local and state permits
• Insurance renewals — general liability, workers' compensation, vehicle
• Federal and state tax filing deadlines
• Health, fire and safety inspections
• Staff certifications and training records
• Registered agent and annual report filings
• Any recurring document with an expiry date

REMINDERS THAT ARRIVE IN TIME

Renewal paperwork takes longer to gather than a single last-minute alert allows. Obligio reminds you 30 days out, again at 14 and 7, and once more the day before. Enough notice to act, and a final catch if you deferred it.

SUGGESTED OBLIGATIONS FOR YOUR STATE AND INDUSTRY

Tell Obligio your location and industry and it suggests the obligations that commonly apply — each one linked to the authority it comes from, with the date it was last reviewed, so you can check the source rather than take our word for it. You choose which to track and set your own dates, because the real deadline depends on your entity type and filing history.

KEEP THE EVIDENCE WITH THE DEADLINE

Upload the certificate, policy or permit against the obligation it proves. When an inspector asks, it is where you expect it to be.

PRIVACY

Obligio contains no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting software. We do not track you across other apps or websites and we do not sell personal information.

NOT LEGAL OR TAX ADVICE

Obligio is an organizational tool. It tracks dates you enter and suggests obligations that commonly apply; it does not file anything on your behalf and it is not a law firm, an accounting firm or a registered agent service. Requirements vary by entity type, location and circumstance, and they change. Always confirm your own deadlines with the relevant authority.
